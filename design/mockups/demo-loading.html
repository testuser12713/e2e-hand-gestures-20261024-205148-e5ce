Three steps, all in the browser

How it works

  1. 01

    Webcam

    The camera is requested only after you press start or the G key, and released when you stop.

  2. 02

    Hand landmarks

    MediaPipe finds 21 points on a single hand, about 30 times per second.

  3. 03

    Command

    The recogniser turns palm movement into a gesture; the adapter calls reveal.js.
