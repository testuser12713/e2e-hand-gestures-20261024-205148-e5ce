HandGestures · reveal.js demo

Hand gestures for presentations

Swipe in front of your webcam to move through this deck. Hands are recognised on your device; no video leaves the browser.

Press G or use the button in the corner to start. Swipe left, right, up or down to navigate. Hold an open palm to pause, a fist for the overview.

Three steps, all in the browser

How it works

  1. 01

    Webcam

    The camera is requested only after you press start or the G key, and released when you stop.

  2. 02

    Hand landmarks

    MediaPipe finds 21 points on a single hand, about 30 times per second.

  3. 03

    Command

    The recogniser turns palm movement into a gesture; the adapter calls reveal.js.

Vertical sub-slide

Mirrored, with a cooldown

  • Swipes follow the preview

    Move your hand to the left in the mirrored picture and the deck goes forward. invertSwipe flips it.

  • One gesture at a time

    After a gesture fires, nothing else fires for cooldownMs (800 by default), so the return stroke is ignored.

  • Slow drift does nothing

    A short warm-up after the hand appears and a minimum speed keep casual movement from changing slides.

Pick one with the adapter option

Adapters

  • revealAdapter(Reveal)

    reveal.js

    Calls next, prev, up, down, togglePause and toggleOverview directly.

  • keyboardAdapter(mapping)

    Any keyboard tool

    Dispatches arrow keys, B and O to the document. The mapping can be overridden.

  • { handle(gesture) }

    Your own

    Any object with a handle method receives every gesture and its timestamp.

Privacy

On-device only

  • Nothing leaves the browser

    No video frame and no landmark is stored or sent anywhere. There is no telemetry.

  • Only model files are fetched

    Requests go to assetBaseUrl for the MediaPipe wasm and model, and only after you start. It accepts https: or relative URLs.

  • The default CDN sees your IP

    Self-host the assets and point assetBaseUrl to them to avoid that.

Classic script or ES module

Add it to your own deck

<!-- works from file:// -->
<script src="dist/hand-gestures.iife.js"></script>
<script>
  HandGestures.createGestureControl({
    adapter: HandGestures.revealAdapter(Reveal)
  });
</script>
// bundled project
import { createGestureControl, keyboardAdapter }
  from "./dist/hand-gestures.es.js";

Without a camera the deck keeps working with keyboard and mouse.
